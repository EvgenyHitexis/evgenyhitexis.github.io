Privacy policy

Evgeny Reader Backup is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account.

  • It uses only the Google Drive drive.file permission, so it can see only the files it created itself, not the rest of the Drive.
  • It stores encrypted backups in the owner's own Drive. The data is encrypted before upload.
  • It shares no data with anyone, sells nothing, and has no other users.
  • The owner can revoke its access at any time in the Google account's security settings.
